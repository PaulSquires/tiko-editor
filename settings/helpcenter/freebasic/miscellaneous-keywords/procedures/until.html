Untilkeyword

Conditional clause used in Do..Loop statements.

Miscellaneous Keywordskeyworddocumented

Syntax

Do Until condition
Loop Until condition

Description


Until is used with the Do...Loop structure.

Remarks

Differences from QB


  • None

See also


  • Do...Loop



Example


Dim a As Integer



a = 1

Do

    Print "hello"

a = a + 1

Loop Until a > 10



'This will continue to print "hello" on the screen until the condition (a > 10) is met.
